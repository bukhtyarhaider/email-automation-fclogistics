New Mailbox Warmup

                        
High delays (4–8 min) & small batches with long rests. Protects new domains against early reputation burn.

                        

                          4–8m gaps • 10/hr • 15–30m rest

Balanced Human Pace

                        
Realistic sales rep pacing (2.5–5 min) with irregular intervals & 10–20 min rests. Standard for daily outreach.

                        

                          2.5–5m gaps • 18/hr • 10–20m rest
                        

                        
                      


                      
                      

                        

                          High Volume
                          120 / day
                        

                        
Aged / Established Domain

                        
Faster pace (1.5–3 min) for warmed mailboxes with strong SPF/DKIM/DMARC and seasoned sender score.

                        

                          1.5–3m gaps • 25/hr • 8–15m rest
                        

                        
                      


                      
                      

                        

                          All-Day Trickle
                          50 / day
                        

                        
Stealth Background

                        
Ultra-gentle trickle (5–10 min) running quietly across an 8–10 hour workday without triggering rate spikes.

                        

                          5–10m gaps • 8/hr • 15–25m rest
                        

                        
                      

                    


                    
                    

                      
                      

                        Spam & Rate-Limit Protections: Randomized gaps prevent ESP bot-fingerprinting. Rolling hourly & daily caps protect domain reputation from blacklists. Periodic rest pauses simulate natural human breaks.
